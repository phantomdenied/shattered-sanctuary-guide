Shattered Sanctuary

A full Diablo-inspired RPG, playable entirely through Discord slash commands — no app, no download, nothing to install. This guide covers everything from your first hunt to the endless Tower.

Run /town in Discord to create your hero — you'll be fighting within a minute.

🗡️Getting Started

Everything begins with one command. No account, no setup — just pick a class and go.

Run /town for the first time and you'll be asked to choose a class: Warrior, Mage, or Rogue. Each has exactly one passive ability — there are no skill trees to plan around, so the difference is felt in your very first fight, not buried in a menu. If a friend gave you a referral code, /town also takes a referral option the first time you run it — enter it before picking a class.

⚔️Warrior
Iron Skin — takes 20% less damage from every hit

Thick skin, heavy hits. Hard to kill, simple to play.

🔥Mage
Arcane Might — deals 10% more damage with every attack

Highest damage output. Squishier than the rest, but hits hard enough to matter.

🗡️Rogue
Deathmark — 25% chance to land a critical hit for double damage

Balanced and unpredictable. Every hit might be a crit.

Class choice is permanent unless you delete your character with /reset and start fresh — there's no respec. Once you've created a hero, /town always takes you straight to the hub instead of the class picker.

🏘️The Town Hub

/town is home base — every system in the game branches out from here, and it always shows your live Stamina bar, Gold, and current Torment setting up top.

Main rows

  • Services — Shops, Quest Board, Arena

The Shops sub-hub

The Shops button opens six more destinations: the gear/potion Shop, the Gambler's Den, the Recruitment Center, the Enchanter, Pet care, and the Inn.

A "Return to Town" button appears on nearly every screen, so you're never more than one click from the hub.

⚔️Hunt & Dungeon

These are the two core loops — quick fights that pay out XP, gold, and gear.

🗡️/hunt

One fight against a random monster. Costs 1 stamina, always. Can drop gear (any slot), a Horadric Shard, a Stamina Gem or Potion, or a Stone of Jordan — and rarely a Treasure Goblin or a tougher hunt boss shows up instead of a normal monster. Hunt loot never rolls legendary rarity — that's a dungeon-only reward.

🏰/dungeon

Three waves plus a boss, back to back — your HP carries over between fights with no healing in between, so a rough wave 1 can cost you the whole run. Costs more stamina than a hunt, and more again at higher Torment (check your own /profile for your exact cost). Can drop gear of any rarity, including legendary and boss-specific Uniques.

Loot decisions

Every gear drop shows what you'd be trading up (or down) from and asks you to Equip or Scrap for gold — your call either way. The button that's actually the smarter pick lights up green so it's obvious at a glance, but nothing is ever forced on you.

Gear tiers (+1 to +4)

Stacked on top of rarity rather than replacing it — a permanent boost the Enchanter (/enchant) can push higher on gear you already own. Each tier is gated behind having unlocked the matching Torment level, and the screen tells you exactly what you need. Max tier is +4.

Horadric Shards & Fragments

A Horadric Shard instantly upgrades your weakest equipped slot to the next rarity tier the moment it drops from a hunt or dungeon — no decision needed. Once every slot you own is already Legendary, a Shard re-forges your most out-of-date weapon or armor at your current level instead, so it's never wasted. A Cautious Adventure pays out Fragments instead of a whole Shard; collect enough and the Enchanter lets you choose which slot to spend the assembled Shard on, instead of it always hitting your weakest. A Dangerous Adventure skips Fragments entirely and pays a real Shard outright on a win.

✨Uniques

Each dungeon boss (and Diablo himself) can drop its own signature weapon or armor — legendary stats plus a permanent combat passive found nowhere else. Weapon and armor only; rings and necklaces stay non-combat. Like any drop, a Unique is always your own Equip-or-Scrap choice, never automatic.

⚡Stamina & The Inn

Stamina replaces cooldowns entirely — it's the one resource every action spends.

You start with 8 max stamina, and your max grows by 1 every level. It regenerates on its own over time, getting faster the higher your level — your exact "+1 in X minutes" countdown is always visible on /profile.

🧪Stamina Potion

Restores 5 stamina instantly — or converts to gold on the spot if you're already full, so it's never wasted.

💎Stamina Gem

Permanently raises your max stamina by 1. One socket per gear piece, so four is the cap.

The Inn

Can't wait for regen? /inn lets you pay gold to fast-track it — priced by how much stamina you're actually missing, not a flat fee. It's not instant either; you're booking a stay, and your stamina arrives after a wait shown live once you check in.

🔥Torment

An optional difficulty dial — /torment — that trades risk for reward.

Six settings, Normal through Torment 5, unlocking gradually as you level. Your own /torment screen always shows exactly when your next tier becomes available. Every step up makes hunt and dungeon monsters (including dungeon bosses) tougher, and pays out more XP and gold in return.

  • Hunt's stamina cost never changes with Torment, but its Horadric Shard chance climbs the higher you go.
  • Dungeon's stamina cost increases by 1 for every Torment level on top of the base cost.
  • The World Boss is unaffected by your Torment setting — it already scales with your whole party.

🕋The Tower

/tower — a solo, single-run climb with no ceiling and no way to bank progress mid-run.

One battle per floor, HP carrying over just like a dungeon's waves — except the Tower never stops getting harder. There is no death penalty: losing costs you nothing, unlike a dungeon defeat. There's also no XP, gold, or loot at any point — the Tower pays in bragging rights only. Your best floor ever is saved permanently and feeds the Tower Leaderboard. A climb costs stamina once, not per floor.

Beat your own personal best and the whole server hears about it — a fresh record posts publicly with your new leaderboard rank.

🗺️Adventures

/adventure sends your hero out on their own, away from you for a while — pick how much to commit and how much risk to take, then wait.

Tap Send on Adventure and a prompt asks how much stamina to commit — up to 30. More stamina buys a better reward tier and a faster trip; the screen shows exactly what a given amount gets you before you confirm anything. Stamina committed beyond 30 stops buying speed, but isn't wasted — see Dangerous below.

Then you choose a path:

🛡️Cautious

The safe bet. A small chance of finding a shortcut shaves time off the trip, plus a chance at a Horadric Shard Fragment on return. Can never end badly.

⚔️Dangerous

Your hero fights a real boss on the way back, scaled to their own power — no mercenary allowed to help. Win, and they come back with everything Cautious pays plus a guaranteed Horadric Shard (a whole one, not a Fragment); commit stamina past 30 and there's a chance at extra Shards and bonus gold on top. Lose, and the trip still takes just as long, but they come back with nothing to show for it.

Hunt, Dungeon, and Tower are locked while your hero is away (everything else in Town still works fine), and there's no canceling once they've left. A live countdown shows how much longer to wait. Flip on the Return Ping toggle and the bot will DM-ping you the moment they're back, so you don't have to keep checking.

😈World Boss & Goblin King

Two server-wide events, both party-based, both triggered by everyone's play adding up together.

😈Diablo, Lord of Terror

Stones of Jordan — a rare drop from hunts and dungeons — count toward a server-wide total, tracked live in /worldboss. The moment the count is hit, Diablo spawns immediately, no warning. Up to 4 heroes can party up and take him on for XP, gold, gear, and a shot at his exclusive Unique, Diablo's Fang. A loss costs nothing — World Boss defeats are free, unlike a dungeon death. A "Notify Me" button lets you opt into a ping for the next spawn.

👑The Treasure Goblin King

Every Treasure Goblin actually defeated (from a hunt or a dungeon) counts toward a second, lighter server-wide total. Hitting it summons the King immediately — trivially easy, gold-only reward, and bringing more friends means more gold each, not a smaller split. No death penalty here either.

📋Quest Board

/questboard — a rotating set of gold-paying objectives.

Four daily objectives reset every 24 hours: Win hunts, Clear dungeons, Spend gold, and Play the Gambler's Den. One weekly objective (drawn from that same pool) resets every 7 days and pays out more. Every quest shows a live progress bar and its gold reward, plus a countdown to the next reset. Clearing every daily objective several days running builds a streak, shown right on the board — keep it going for a little extra gold on top.

🧙The Greedy Mage

/mage — a pure gold sink with no payoff for the donor, on purpose.

Give the Mage gold and it's gone — nothing comes back to you personally. What it does instead is build toward a server-wide reward: once the community's running total (shown live) crosses its threshold, Mother's Blessing activates for everyone — +50% gold and XP on every hunt, dungeon, and World Boss kill, for as long as it's active. Both the start and the end of a Blessing post as public announcements, so you'll always know when one's running and how long is left. Gold lost to a death penalty, or surrendered when you Rebirth, also feeds the same total — so even a rough run isn't entirely wasted for the server.

🌀Rebirth

The endgame reset — trade your progress for a permanent edge and start climbing again.

Once you've reached the level shown on your own /profile, a Rebirth option appears there. Confirming trades your character back to level 1 in exchange for a permanent boost to XP gain and base combat stats — bigger with each Rebirth, but by less every time, so it never fully replaces playing the game normally.

📉You lose

Your level, all equipped gear (and its tiers/gems), your mercenary, your Torment setting, and any Horadric Shard Fragments. Your gold is surrendered to the Greedy Mage.

📌You keep

Your pet (at its current tier) and every lifetime record — hunts won, dungeons cleared, Tower best floor, duel record, Goblin King kills, gold donated, referrals, and every achievement and title you've earned.

Permanent, with no undo — the confirmation screen shows your exact current and next-Rebirth numbers before you commit.

🎲Gambler's Den & Arena

Two ways to wager gold against the house — or against another hero.

🪙Coin Flip — /gamble

Wager gold on a flip. Win, and you double it; lose, and it's gone.

🔮Seer's Wheel — /guessnumber

Guess a number from 1 to 10 — genuine 1-in-10 odds. Guess right and your wager quadruples.

The Arena — /duel

Challenge another hero to a wagered fight. You each pick one of three moves in secret — Attack beats Magic, Magic beats Block, Block beats Attack — and both picks stay hidden until the target accepts and locks theirs in too. An unanswered challenge expires after 24 hours with nothing lost.

It isn't a coin flip or a guaranteed win: your move choice and how your character compares to theirs both nudge the odds, but the underdog always has a real shot. The result screen always shows your exact odds for that specific fight after it resolves. Winner takes the whole pot — both wagers combined.

🏕️Recruits & Pets

Recruitment Center — /recruit

Hire a mercenary for a temporary combat edge — the exact bonus is always shown live before you hire. It isn't permanent: each merc is good for a limited number of hunts and dungeons before they move on, and hiring a replacement while one's still active drops the old one with no refund for unused charges.

Pets — /pet

Purely cosmetic — every pet earns gold at the same passive rate just by existing, no fighting or stamina involved, so pick whichever one you like. Gold stockpiles while you're away until you collect it in person, capped at a maximum. Upgrading a pet (up to tier 5) raises that cap, not how fast it earns.

🐺 Shadow HoundA loyal wolf that shadows your steps, snatching up stray coin as you travel. 👹 Coin ImpA small imp with a nose for gold and absolutely no interest in a fight. 🦇 Cave BatFlits through the dark corners of the Sanctuary, dropping whatever it finds. 🐦‍⬛ Carrion CrowPicks the battlefield clean of coin long after you've moved on. 🐍 Sand ViperBurrows into forgotten ruins and drags back whatever glitters.

🎟️Referrals & Shop

Bring a friend

Your personal referral code is shown on your own /profile. Share it, and when a friend enters it the first time they run /town and later reaches level 5, you get 500 gold — up to 5 friends per referrer. Your profile tracks exactly how many of those five you've earned so far.

The Shop — /shop

Spend gold instead of waiting on drops: a gear roll for any slot (priced the same across weapon/armor/ring/necklace, scaling with your level), Stamina Potions, and Stamina Gems. Every price is shown live before you buy, with a confirmation step since purchases can't be undone.

🏆Titles & Achievements
